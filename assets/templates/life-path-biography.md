# Locked life-path biography

Write the locked-path pre-story life as a readable causal biography. Keep its branch, candidate hash, lock revision, biography revision, and causal-node references available for provenance, but do not turn the prose into a résumé, questionnaire, or expanded form. The path lock authorizes this expansion; it does not approve facts added here. Record the author's separate approval against the exact finished body hash in the workbench.

## Fact boundary and opening condition

State the story-canon facts and the permitted creative space. Preserve intentional unknowns and contradictions.

## Life stages

Follow the character's actual major stages through the moment before the story opens. Within each stage, connect environment and relationships to perception, interpretation, choice, cost, feedback, updates, and residues. Include ordinary life as well as turning points.

## Relationship and shared-history arcs

Trace how important relationships and shared contexts formed, changed, frayed, repaired, or remained misunderstood. Keep each person's memory version distinct.

## Arrival state

Compile what the person now notices, expects, wants, fears, protects, owes, misreads, does first, refuses to say, and can communicate without words. This is a bridge to runtime assets, not screenplay dialogue.

## Uncertainty and revision conditions

Record unresolved gaps, competing explanations, and new canon that would require an explicit unlock and downstream recompilation.
