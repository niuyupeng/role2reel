# Fictional character life-path readings

> **Mode: `explore`.** These are authored possibilities for a fictional life, not facts, probabilities, diagnoses, or a production lock. Their order is not a ranking. Nothing below enters downstream canon until the author chooses a route and separately approves a production lock.

## Fixed ground

### What the story has established

State only supplied or author-locked facts in ordinary language.

### What is visible at the opening

List observable behavior, circumstances, relationships, objects, and claims without treating interpretations as facts.

### What may be invented

Name the permitted creative space and any boundaries.

### What can remain unknown

Keep gaps that do not need an answer.

## Reading 1 — causal title

**The life in one movement:** Summarize how this route reaches the fixed present without reducing it to a trait.

**How the route unfolds:** Connect the consequential stages, environments, relationships, interpretations, choices, pressures, feedback, and revisions.

**The ordinary years:** Show the repetition, routine, plateau, compromise, or relationship pattern that made the route stick.

**Agency and circumstance:** Show the actual mixture of pursuit, avoidance, support, chance, pressure, coercion, and refused alternatives. Omit any force that does not belong.

**The hidden bill:** Name the cost, debt, dependence, missed route, concealment, or unfinished repair that a public summary would miss.

**The story they tell themself / what remains unsettled:** Separate self-account, available evidence, other people's possible versions, and intentional unknowns.

**What survives into the opening:** Describe learned expectations, attention, relationship strategy, taboo, retrieval cues, and unresolved residue.

**First impulse before explanation:** State what the character notices, checks, protects, avoids, or does before reaching for technique or speech. Note any line that an action, gaze, distance, delay, or silence can replace.

**What this route changes:** Explain the effect on theme, arc pressure, key relationships, and believable present choices.

## Neutral comparison

Compare the routes by causal movement, ordinary-life pattern, agency/circumstance mixture, hidden bill, self-story gap, opening attention, first impulse, relationship move, and story consequence. Do not score them, estimate which is true, or recommend a winner unless the author separately supplies a creative criterion and asks for that comparison.

## Author decision menu

The author may:

- select one reading as-is for further development;
- edit any part of a reading;
- splice compatible parts into a newly reconciled reading;
- reject one or all readings;
- regenerate along a new causal constraint or divergence axis;
- keep any cause, period, or event intentionally unknown.

Record the author's words or project-source reference. Do not infer a choice from silence, praise, or the model's preference.
