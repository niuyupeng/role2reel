# Role2Reel

Build the person before the line, the scene before the shot, and the shot before the model prompt. Role2Reel combines two systems rather than replacing one with the other: the existing humanization-to-character-to-scene-to-storyboard-to-video direction chain, and **命线反演** (fictional life-path reverse inference), a visible fictional-prehistory exploration layer that feeds that entire chain.

## Preserve the dependency order

For end-to-end work, use this dependency order and skip irrelevant stages:

For an already selected direction being repaired, or an explicit single-character growth-path experiment, follow [the growth-review boundary](references/biography-growth-review.md) instead of restarting stage 3's unresolved-choice exploration. A request to review a path stops before stage 4 until the author asks for expansion.

1. Establish the deliverable, audience, language, duration, and supplied facts. Ask only when a missing choice would materially change the result.
2. Build a meaning ledger before rewriting substantial rough material. Preserve claim ownership, uncertainty, chronology, and emotional function; keep created fiction separate from faithful transformation.
3. When character history is underdetermined and consequential, run 命线反演 in two layers. In author-facing `explore`, read sparse present traces as at least three genuinely different possible lives, including a counter-reading and mixed human causality; show what each path explains, fails to explain, would leave behind, and would make the character notice or do first at story opening. Diversify the candidate set at the level of primary causal engine, social ecology, ordinary-life pattern, self-story, hidden bill, and present strategy; do not repeatedly fall back on one secret incident, caregiving debt, or “nothing happened, time drifted” route. Do not rank a winner. Let the author select, edit, splice compatible parts, reject all, regenerate, or keep gaps unknown. In backstage `production-lock`, materialize the chosen route against a stable character/package identity and versioned fact boundary, then bind the explicit author decision to the exact snapshot, candidate content, branch, and lock revision.
4. Expand only the author-locked route for canon-bound production. When the author explicitly selects a route and requests its biography, begin an author-review draft under the draft-expansion contract in [references/life-paths.md](references/life-paths.md); do not demand repeated selection or fabricate a formal lock. For a consequential `deep` character, build a causally continuous opening-before life—ordinary years, reversals, relationships, work, avoidance, effort, cost, self-story, and present residue—not a résumé. Depth is judged by causal, relationship, ordinary-life, contradiction, and scene-usefulness coverage, not a default page or character quota. Before formal canon compilation, the author separately approves that exact biography revision and body hash.
5. Compile only the locked candidate and exact author-approved biography into **two distinct libraries**: a cognitive-resource library that constrains what the person can notice, know, retrieve, infer, misread, value, and expect; and a speech corpus that constrains how this person expresses or suppresses something with this relationship, goal, and pressure. Also compile episodic memory, knowledge boundaries, shared propositions, second-order beliefs, relationship history, current need, risk, and available strategies. Bind runtime artifacts to the approved source closure and invalidate them when a bound value changes.
6. Review the outline against the locked life. Surface incompatibilities and offer author-controlled repair options; do not silently rewrite canon or force the person to serve a beat. Then define the scene contract and information asymmetry, keeping objective events separate from what each character remembers or believes.
7. Simulate consequential exchanges in two retrieval passes: cue and literal read; comprehension retrieval; private interpretation and pragmatic attribution; inference and belief update; judgment, stance, and social objective; then expression retrieval, suppression/modulation, and a chosen action, gaze, pause, silence, or minimum necessary line. Preserve loaded words such as “这次/又/已经/我们” as evidence rather than paraphrasing away their presupposition. Let occupational expertise supply means only after the human appraisal. Let relationship history change what is already trusted, what still needs testing, who can be interrupted, how much can be risked, and therefore the tactic—not merely the sentence style. Treat corpus features as permitted options, not a checklist that must all appear. Let the other person's observation update the next turn. In a controlled first-response comparison, keep the supplied cue identical and do not invent an interlocutor reply before the compared outputs. When the user explicitly asks for a compact runtime, keep each middle state visible as a compact field—including an explicit `no_change` belief delta when nothing changes—rather than collapsing the chain into “current judgment.” Cite supplied cognitive and speech resources by their IDs; if the source has no IDs, assign clearly temporary scene-local refs instead of returning untraceable paraphrases.
8. Surface only what this person would actually say, do, look at, withhold, misdirect, or leave silent. Then run a mute pass: remove every drafted line and replay the beat. Restore a line only if its absence changes what the other person can now choose, know, refuse, or do. A line cap is a ceiling, never a quota; do not refill it with a command that duplicates the action or a sentence that states the relationship principle the behavior already shows.
9. Map beats as changes in information, emotion, power, intention, or physical state.
10. Before timing a production storyboard, run the source-truth gate against the actual screenplay/outline and then the atomic-shot gate. Do not repair a compound row by merely making it longer; split it or record an author-approved continuous-shot cluster.
11. Calculate dialogue, action, visual, camera, and performance-hold floors; keep performance, editorial, and provider clocks separate; flag infeasible compression instead of silently changing the beat. Review environment-specific sound carriers, force sources, state handoffs, and exact text overlays before generation.
12. Export final shot text in the fixed copy-paste contract: `shotN｜`, `景别与运镜：`, `拍摄方式：`, `画面与动线：`, then contiguous time-coded beats. Preserve original source shot IDs and any intentional gaps while allowing local labels to restart per generation segment.
13. Translate the locked scene and shots into exactly one provider/task mode with deny-by-default reference borrowing. The adapter must not rewrite the upstream story.
14. Audit the requested deliverable at its own level. Do not claim that schemas or linting prove artistic quality, actor playability, or adoption rate.

## Non-negotiable invariants

Apply each invariant only to the stage named. A narrow fidelity task such as typo-only correction does not inherit scene-generation requirements.

- **Source fidelity precedes polish.** Do not turn hesitation into certainty, merge distinct speakers, or invent supporting facts.
- **Character construction — an endpoint is not a life path.** Occupation, education, status, awards, and identity categories may constrain access, expertise, resources, or pressure; they do not independently establish personality, morality, speech, or first response.
- **命线反演 exploration — possibility must remain plural and visible.** Treat the same present as compatible with different causal lives. Include a non-obvious counter-reading; vary the path's mechanism, cost, relationship history, self-story, and opening impulse rather than renaming credentials or traits. Do not assign probabilities or let the model choose canon.
- **命线反演 safety — fictional hypotheses are not recovered truth.** Do not infer sensitive history about a real person, and do not present creative reverse inference as divination, diagnosis, profiling, investigation, or evidence.
- **Life-path development — candidates are not canon.** Only an author-locked branch may enter formal canon-bound character, memory, relationship, outline, scene, turn-state, or screenplay artifacts. Rejected branches must not leak downstream. Explicitly requested exploratory scripts may contain clearly labeled scene-local proposals, not silently approved biography facts; do not claim that such a draft completes the deep-character workflow.
- **Life-path development — lock records require an actual author decision.** Do not fill `author_decision`, `tier_author_decision`, or `author_lock` from model preference, scoring, or repeated use. Record the user's or project's explicit decision source and bind it to the exact character ID, stable package context, branch, fact-boundary hash, candidate hash, and lock revision; static validation can check consistency but cannot prove who made the decision.
- **Life-path development — path lock is not biography approval.** Expansion may add concrete facts that were absent from the causal skeleton. Do not compile them until the author approves the exact character/package identity, branch, fact snapshot, candidate hash, path revision, biography revision, and body hash; any bound change invalidates that approval.
- **Life-path development — depth cannot be claimed by length alone.** A consequential character marked `deep` requires the causal, temporal, relationship, evidence, and present-residue coverage in [references/life-paths.md](references/life-paths.md). There is no default page or character minimum. Requests such as “四十页/四万字，意思是丰富” are qualitative depth requests, not numerical gates. Repetition and random trivia cannot substitute for useful history.
- **Life-path evaluation — keep the evidence tier visible.** When an author-approved `light` path is used only to test behavior, label the delivered artifact `evaluation_scope: light behavior fixture; not deep-biography evidence` (or a clear Chinese equivalent). Keep this as production metadata, not dialogue. A convincing light scene must never be reported as evidence that the deep-biography, full compilation, or adoption workflow succeeded.
- **Character and scene work — rich private model, restrained public line.** A character may interpret deeply without explaining that interpretation aloud. Do not turn debt, evaluation risk, knowledge boundary, attribution, and intended delay into a spoken checklist merely because the runtime records them.
- **Character and scene work — event, memory, and belief are different.** Never leak author knowledge or another character's secret into a line.
- **Character construction — cognition and language are separate resources.** Retrieve experience, knowledge, relationship, and belief to understand the cue before retrieving speech evidence to choose expression. A catchphrase cannot replace a judgment, and a biography cannot justify giving every character the same authorial voice.
- **Explicit CharacterOS runtime — do not skip the middle.** When runtime state is requested, show compact evidence references, private interpretation, pragmatic attribution, inference, belief/confidence delta, judgment, stance, social objective, expression references, suppression/output, and consequence in that order. These are inspectable state records, not hidden free-form reasoning.
- **Scene work — test the body and relationship before dialogue.** Try action, distance, gaze, stopping, expression, pause, silence, and established shared context first. After drafting, mute each line in turn. If the same state change and next choice remain legible, delete it. Keep speech only when it performs work the visible behavior cannot—such as supplying unavailable information, asking for a decision, setting a necessary boundary, or changing another person's available tactic. A requested maximum is not a target; never fill spare lines with instructions that reduce the other person's agency or with a spoken summary of the relationship strategy.
- **Scene work — shared history is compressed, not recapped or invented.** A nonverbal cue or shorthand may retrieve only previously established mutual knowledge. Permission to interrupt, omit a subject, or use a half-line does not authorize a newly invented code word. Mutually informed characters do not explain shared history to one another for the audience.
- **Dramatic scene or dialogue creation — every exchange has a consequence.** It may change power, intimacy, obligation, certainty, exposure, or the next available tactic; it need not add a new fact. Do not apply this as a rewrite mandate during typo-only correction or faithful cleanup.
- **Storyboard work — cut on state or audience need, not punctuation.** A silent reaction may deserve a shot; a long coherent judgment may remain in one shot.
- **Storyboard timing — clocks are not interchangeable.** There is no universal shot duration. The provider's minimum generation length does not become the editorial cut length; declare long-generation/short-trim or split the duty when necessary. A time-code block must be contiguous and must not hide a missing reaction, unreadable reveal, or incomplete contact/result path.
- **Storyboard source truth — upstream facts are locked before polish.** Preserve speaker, dialogue ownership, purposeful repetition, plot action, and causal setup. A source mismatch, meaningful drift, unapproved addition, or unapproved shot cluster blocks production lock; do not hide it inside a polished prompt.
- **Storyboard readiness — atomicity precedes duration.** Decide whether a row is atomic, split-required, split-recommended, or an author-approved cluster before estimating seconds. Never inflate a compound row to avoid a split, and never let the provider clock rewrite the editorial shot.
- **Physical/sound/text continuity — the medium and force must be named.** Do not use generic wind or airborne sound in a vacuum without an explicit stylized rule; state what moves dust, liquid, fabric, or debris; track prop/UI/position/axis/sound handoffs; mark exact screen text `POST_COMPOSITE_TEXT` unless an approved text-capable stage owns it.
- **Storyboard and video-direction work — camera movement needs a narrative or perceptual motive.** Static framing and negative space are valid choices.
- **Storyboard and video-direction work — cause precedes response.** Preserve stimulus-to-understanding-to-reaction timing and contact-to-force-to-result physics.
- **Video adaptation — reference inheritance is deny-by-default.** Borrow only named properties for named targets and intervals. Identity, wardrobe, setting, camera, performance, sound, music, text, and style from a source stay excluded unless explicitly authorized.
- **Two-source transitions protect three different spans.** Bind A's protected source span, the output bridge window, and B's protected source span separately; endpoint frames alone are not protected intervals, and the bridge may not leak unlisted properties across sources.
- **Multi-panel order is not permission to decorate.** Bind and exclude properties panel by panel, map every panel to a shot or exact interval, and explicitly deny unrequested CTA, logo, and decorative effects even when the general reference policy is already deny-by-default. Carry any input-specific bans on dialogue, narration, music, or camera movement into the task rather than leaving them implicit.
- **Provider syntax is downstream.** Confirm current model capabilities when limits or modes matter; do not rely on remembered product specifications.
- **Do not expose hidden chain-of-thought.** If production notes help, provide compact evidence, state, and decision fields rather than private step-by-step reasoning.

## Work with incomplete material

For life-path work in an existing story universe, first apply [world grounding](references/world-grounding.md) and the source checkpoint in [references/fatecasting.md](references/fatecasting.md): read the author's actual screenplay/world material before inventing history. Never replace it with game-role stereotypes or unapproved assistant drafts. World research must change feasible choices, ordinary life, and relationship costs, not merely the place names.

Make reversible assumptions when they do not alter authorship, plot truth, or production constraints, and label them briefly. When a sparse endpoint admits several plausible histories, provide clearly separated candidates rather than silently choosing the stereotype. If missing information would change character knowledge, branch selection, scene outcome, duration, or provider mode, ask one focused question or preserve the alternatives.

## Use the included resources

- Resolve bundled scripts relative to this `SKILL.md`, not relative to the user's current project directory. Initialize a project without overwriting existing files:

  `python <role2reel-skill-dir>/scripts/init_project.py <project-dir> --characters "A,B" --scene scene-001`

- Use `scripts/audit_humanization.py` before a production-bound rewrite travels downstream; it opens the source, ledger, and draft and checks exact identity, revision, hash, and approval bindings without claiming semantic fidelity. Treat `scripts/audit_dialogue.py` as a warning-oriented linter, not a taste judge. Modern `scripts/audit_storyboard.py` runs must receive the actual meaning, scene, beat, and visual-bible files; legacy v0.1 boards retain structural-only checking. Use `scripts/audit_character_runtime.py` to check that explicit CharacterOS turns bind comprehension and expression resources in separate stages. Use `scripts/audit_continuity.py` and `scripts/audit_video_task.py` for their declared structural contracts. None of these tools can judge psychological truth, actor playability, or cinematic quality.
- Use `scripts/audit_life_paths.py` to check fact-boundary records and hashes, branch-lock decision bindings, exact duplicate spines, separate biography approval, exact compiled-file hashes and provenance closure, conditional outline review, automatically declared shared-event ledgers, and the deep-biography mechanical contract. It cannot prove author identity, detect unmarked semantic leakage, judge causal density, or establish artistic truth.
- Use `scripts/audit_clipboard_prompts.py` on the text export to check the fixed paste-ready shot syntax, field order, time-code origin, contiguity, duplicate labels, and obvious unfinished text. It cannot judge cinematic quality, natural performance, or whether a provider will render the intended action.
- Use `scripts/audit_shot_readiness.py` on a source-bound readiness record to check source comparison fields, atomicity/release blockers, hard-floor feasibility, vacuum sound-carrier conflicts, continuity states, and post-composite text plans. It cannot establish that a source comparison is semantically correct or that a simulated review was human evidence.
- Use `scripts/audit_staged_eval.py` before changing an FT-14 result to `behavior_pass: true`. It re-runs the mechanical life-path audit and checks the exact files, hashes, task separation, variants, traceability, and review record; it cannot prove that the recorded tasks or reviews actually occurred.
- Use files under `assets/templates/` as starting contracts. Adapt them to the user's material; do not fill unknowns with fabricated facts.

## Deliver cleanly

Return the artifact the user requested, followed by only the assumptions, warnings, or audit notes needed to use it. Do not dump every intermediate ledger by default. For an end-to-end package, use the layered formats in [references/output-contracts.md](references/output-contracts.md).
