# World-grounded character development

Use this reference when lives or scenes depend on an existing fictional universe. The outcome is a usable, source-bounded world basis, not an encyclopedic word quota. A narrow scene with sufficient established canon does not require a new franchise-wide investigation.

## Establish which continuity is being written

Read the author's actual source and corrections first, using the source checkpoint in [fatecasting.md](fatecasting.md). Name the installment, adaptation, platform, release/version, and period when these affect a claim. Keep publication dates separate from in-world dates.

Maintain two distinct questions: **what the external IP establishes**, and **what this author has established for this adaptation**. An explicit project departure governs this project's writing, but must not be reported as an official franchise fact. External lore cannot silently overwrite the author's screenplay. An earlier assistant invention cannot enter either category through repetition.

Separate species, political affiliation, playable faction, occupation, named individual, costume, and narrative function. A role's victory condition or advertised ability does not establish a named character's childhood, moral nature, suicidal wish, unlimited power, or use of that ability in every adaptation. An institutional conflict can create real lethal danger without making every member psychologically identical.

## Research by claims, not accumulated links

For broad requested research, cover the dimensions that could change the story: origin/conflict, institutions and material life, geography and chronology, technology or supernatural limits, identity and factions, named characters, and unresolved contradictions. Use a coverage inventory to distinguish checked material, partial access, leads, and actual gaps. “All available stories” is a research ambition, not a supportable claim of global completeness.

When delegated research is authorized, split independent source domains and give every researcher the same continuity boundary. Cross-check consequential claims against the source, not against agreement among agents; several summaries of one fan article are one evidence chain. Do not create extra agents as a mandatory skill ritual.

Prefer original works, publisher/developer releases, directly visible game material, and attributable creator statements. A useful source record contains:

- claim and its precise scope;
- original URL or supplied file plus a section, timestamp, frame, or passage locator;
- author/publisher, publication date or version when known;
- what was actually inspected: full relevant text, sampled frames, transcript, search snippet, or second-hand report;
- evidence kind: direct work, official explanation, attributed transcription, community interpretation, author canon, or new proposal;
- limitation/contradiction and the concrete writing consequence.

Do not promote search snippets to full reading, sampled frames to full viewing, a wiki's attribution to access to the original message, or fan-hosted “lore” to official canon. Mark unofficial sections separately even inside otherwise useful compilations. Trace mirrors back to their common source. Cite short supporting evidence rather than copying whole stories or translating entire guides.

An in-world newspaper, propaganda poster, boast, rumor, dream, fourth-wall joke, or unreliable narrator establishes that the statement exists in the work; it may not establish that its entire account is objectively true. Verify visible text before interpreting motives from it. A creator saying there are multiple perspectives is not confirmation of a specific fan theory about who started a war.

## Keep conflicts and missing history visible

For conflicting claims, record both scopes and sources. Check for patches, retcons, separate platforms, alternate continuity, localization, or a statement made as a joke. Later publication alone does not prove a deliberate retcon. If the conflict remains unresolved, preserve alternatives or leave the point unused; do not average contradictory facts into a new authoritative timeline.

Separate **shown event order**, **explicit relative chronology**, and **unknown connections**. A map's launch date is not its fictional date. One alternate-dimension explanation does not place every location in a separate universe. Technological or supernatural examples establish their observed scope, not universally available abilities.

Missing primary material is a named gap, not permission to invent lost lore. Continue work on supported facts. Ask the author only when choosing among incompatible continuities would materially change the deliverable; otherwise mark an optional creative bridge as unapproved. Repeated searching need not continue after relevant independent sources stop adding evidence: report the remaining gaps and research coverage honestly.

## Make the world causally necessary to a life

Before presenting life-path candidates, connect source-grounded conditions to lived choices:

**world pressure or affordance → recurring daily/relationship experience → a choice and its price → learned expectation → present action or hesitation.**

For example, a faction border may affect who can safely travel, repair equipment, be believed, or accept help. Show how this particular person dealt with that condition over time. Do not invent a universal border law just to make the explanation work. A fictional institution or routine added for a candidate remains a proposal, even when it is plausible.

Where hostility is established, do not reduce an attempted killing to friendly banter. Cross-faction cooperation must respond to changed stakes or earned evidence; it need not instantly erase suspicion or public costs. Conversely, do not manufacture a war trauma for every person or turn all humor into a symptom. Ordinary pleasure, vanity, boredom, competence, mutual aid, play, and misjudgment also belong to the world.

Check three boundaries before handing candidates to the author:

1. **World removal:** if replacing the setting with a generic workplace leaves every relevant choice and cost unchanged, the grounding is probably cosmetic. Rebuild the weak causal link, not the proper nouns. This does not require every ordinary habit to be unique to the IP.
2. **Individual variation:** the same faction and circumstances must permit different lives and responses. Use the plurality and counter-reading method in [fatecasting.md](fatecasting.md); research cannot pick the author's branch.
3. **Source re-entry:** the proposed life must return to the actual opening and later established behavior without changing first meetings into old friendships, accidents into secret plans, or uncertain identities into facts. Test against contrary scenes as well as convenient lines.

For each consequential inference, distinguish its world anchor, source-character anchor, and invented bridge. A sparse cameo can keep its allegiance, origin, and purpose unknown. Do not manufacture an elaborate life simply to match the length of better-documented leads.

## Handoff and revision

Deliver a readable world basis with source links, a short contradiction/gap list, and the consequences for the specific characters. Keep detailed research notes available without making the author read a source dump before seeing what changed. Follow [fatecasting.md](fatecasting.md) for candidate choices and [life-paths.md](life-paths.md) for actual canon lock; research completion is neither biography approval nor a finished screenplay.

When a world assumption changes, identify affected candidates, memories, relationships, and scenes. Mark obsolete interpretations as superseded rather than letting both versions remain active. Preserve valid author facts and do not overwrite an unrelated approved branch.

Store franchise research and unpublished manuscript extracts in the project, not as universal defaults in this reusable skill. Public skill changes should improve the method and use synthetic tests; publishing user scripts or private files requires separate authorization. Link resolution and packaging checks prove only artifact integrity, not historical completeness or artistic quality.
