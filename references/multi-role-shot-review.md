# Multi-role shot review

For a high-risk board, use independent review lenses rather than one blended “AI score.” A useful panel is: narrative/editorial, editing/rhythm, cinematography, performance, action/physics, sound/continuity, AI execution, and script supervision. Each lens reports evidence, severity, proposed repair, and whether author confirmation is needed.

This can be a model-simulated review, but it must be labeled as simulated. Do not call role-play output a real expert panel, human blind evaluation, actor test, production adoption result, or probability of success. Keep numeric scores optional; a structured disagreement and first-failure boundary is more useful than a decorative average.

The panel reviews in this order:

1. source truth and dialogue ownership;
2. atomic shot or approved cluster;
3. dynamic timing and reaction/physical capacity;
4. visual identity, axis, prop, sound carrier, and state handoff;
5. provider generation limits and post-composite text;
6. author decision and production-lock status.

When lenses disagree, preserve the disagreement and ask for the smallest author decision that changes the release boundary. A clean mechanical audit never overrides an unresolved P0, an unapproved cluster, or a physical/sound contradiction.
